# Trajectory behavior study

Executed bundles and progress bands describe inter-seed behavior. Rollout clouds describe one intra-step population and its optimizer weights. Occupancy pools recorded states with explicit seed/time weights. It is neither proposal covariance nor a predictive state PDF. Failures remain included. Paired seed/time snapshots can have different current states and warm starts. Pointwise 50/90% bands are descriptive sample quantiles. See docs/trajectory_behavior_findings.md for interpretation and inference limits.
